TECHNOLOGY RESPONSE

1

Sewage → power, soil, wash-water

Heat-and-pressure cook (thermal hydrolysis) + closed digestion. Biogas runs the plant. Sterile compost for parks. Cleaned water for streets. Phosphorus crystals for fertilizer. This is city-grade kit, not a lab.

2

Garbage → benches, kiln fuel, ash

Food scraps join the gas tanks. Clean plastic becomes bins and decking. Dirty mixed plastic is kiln fuel (legal in the EU). Ash becomes road base. Do not bet the island on a plastic-to-oil toy.

3

Concrete → new sidewalks

Crush demolition rubble. Lock CO₂ from the biogas into the gravel. Mix a little sludge-char (2025 tests: 32 MPa, ~⅓ less climate load). Hotel wreckage becomes the next pavement.
