## STRUCTURE (full)

### COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:06
**Visual:** Pre-dawn Crete. Stone path above Rousa Eklisia. Olive dark. A child’s bare feet on cold dirt.  
**Camera:** ground-level track, shallow DOF, film grain.  
**VO (Brother, quiet):**  
“His name is Mano. Rousa Eklisia.”

### THE VILLAGE — 0:06–0:12
**Visual:** Whitewashed walls, church silhouette, mountain spine. Smoke of cooking fires. Occupied sky — foreign trucks far on the road, not close.  
**Camera:** slow rise over rooftops.  
**VO:**  
“Crete did not sleep free. German and Italian boots held the roads.”

### THE BOY — 0:12–0:20
**Visual:** Eight-year-old Greek boy — thin, alert eyes, too-old face. Not smiling. Watching from behind a dry-stone wall.  
**Camera:** close on eyes, then hands.  
**VO:**  
“He was eight. Opposition. Not a soldier on paper — a child of the mountain who chose the side of his people.”

### THE ACT — 0:20–0:36
**Visual (tasteful, no glorified violence):**  
- Night. Supply crates near a parked foreign truck / depot edge.  
- Small hands lift a belt, a box, cartridges wrapped in cloth.  
- He runs low along the wall, breath white in cold air.  
- Passes the load into shadow — to larger hands of the resistance (adult silhouette only).  
**Camera:** tight, tense, handheld micro-shake, blue-black night grade.  
**VO:**  
“He stole ammunition from the German and Italian troops.  
Not for glory. For the fight. For the village. For tomorrow.”

### THE COST — 0:36–0:44
**Visual:** Dawn again. Same path. The boy sits with his back to the wall, exhausted. Church bell far away.  
**Camera:** still frame, wind in olive leaves.  
**VO:**  
“Courage at eight has no medal. Only memory.”

### THE LINE THROUGH TIME — 0:44–0:54
**Visual:** Match-cut: boy’s eyes → old man’s eyes (grandfather Mano, warm light). Hands that once carried shells now break bread. Family table. Aegean light.  
**Camera:** dissolve, soft.  
**VO:**  
“From Rousa Eklisia the bloodline walks forward.  
Astranov carries the name. Brother keeps the watch. SpaceXAI Kitty on the console.”

### END CARD — 0:54–1:00
**Visual:** Sunset over Cretan hills. Single candle. No flag circus.  
**Text (if any, clean white):**  
**MANO · ROUSA EKLISIA**  
*Opposition fighter · age 8*  
*Αιώνια η μνήμη*  
**VO last line:**  
“Grandfather. We remember.”
