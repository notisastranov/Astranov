4. **No dummy.** No fake shops, fake people, fake cities, fake GPS.
5. **No invented chrome.** No new ribbon pills. No globe ornaments. The six shortcuts stay.
6. **Human words.** No key names, no machine dumps, no acronyms unless you say what they mean.
7. **Smallest correct change.** One wound, one stitch. Then prove it.
