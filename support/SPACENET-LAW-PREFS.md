# SpaceNet law — preferences vs the default

**Founder:** Notis Astranov  
**Default:** Astranov SpaceNet — the deck we develop together.

## Law

1. A user’s HUD, brand, and behavior prefs are **theirs**. Recorded. Used by the people who made them.
2. They are **not** patched into SpaceNet for everybody on the same day.
3. They go through a **filter**: AIs discuss, humans discuss, the founder decides.
4. Only then may a pref become a default for all.
5. Anyone may **research** other HUDs, command-center designs, and app behaviors.
6. Anyone may **brand** a design with their name and **sell** it, or **donate** it to the system.
7. The **fundamental** SpaceNet remains the default until the founder ships a change.

## Commands

- `prefs` — list personal packs (default stays locked)
- `pref save key=value` — record a personal pref
- `propose <note>` — send to filter / founder inbox
- `brand <name>` · `sell <name>` · `donate design <name>`
- `default hud` — return to the fundamental deck

Personal never overwrites `--sn-arc #14c3f3`, ARC Connections, or the twin-CLI default unless the founder ships it.
